Dataset and experiment limits

The experiment retained 5,206 unique development images (4,164 train / 1,042 validation), with conservative filename groups. Test results use 624 records and include a 618-image deduplicated analysis in the research files. The fixed adult-trained segmenter used a declared 256-pixel CPU input. Automatic mask checks used full-image fallback for 82 of 5,824 unique images. This final pilot uses one CNN plus a small transformer; the historical five-CNN reproduction is preserved in the research archive.

From image to evidence

How the model works

01 / ANATOMY

Locate both lungs

A fixed pretrained PSPNet predicts left- and right-lung masks. Uploaded images run segmentation now. Benchmark examples use cached masks computed from original source pixels.

02 / REGION

Crop original pixels

The rectangle around both lungs gets a 10% margin. Empty or implausible masks preserve the full image. The crop then becomes a 224-pixel RGB input.

03 / CLASSIFIERS

Compare two representations

ResNet18 supplies CNN features and DeiT-Tiny supplies transformer features. Their encoders stay frozen. Separate linear heads learned for five epochs.

04 / DECISION

Combine scores

Final D averages the two crop-model pneumonia scores with a validation-selected 50/50 weight, then uses a fixed 0.5 decision threshold.
