DEFENCE POINTS

M2: five CNNs, five epochs, final full FP32 checkpoints, original benchmark split.
M4: frozen pretrained ResNet18 and DeiT-Tiny, five classifier-head epochs,
matched full/cropped inputs with a separate group-aware validation split.
These are different training protocols. Compare A/B/C/D within M4. Do not use
M2-to-M4 differences as an isolated crop effect.

The crop prediction was made before implementation. A disappointing crop result
is a valid experimental finding. Report the measured direction and uncertainty.
Validation selects checkpoints and blend weights. Test scores never select them.
The original proposed five-CNN-plus-ViT experiment is larger than this pilot.
A ViT blend weight of zero means validation selected the CNN alone for that
condition; do not claim an effective transformer contribution there.

Masks are model predictions, not manual ground truth. There are no lung/lesion
annotations for segmentation accuracy measurement in this classification set.
The bounding crop includes some non-lung pixels. It does not remove every shortcut.
Grad-CAM is qualitative attribution. It does not prove clinical reasoning.

Reliability limits: one seed, pediatric single-center data, imperfect filename
patient proxies, and a benchmark test already inspected during earlier work.
Do not claim clinical readiness, global novelty, or superiority over papers with
different splits. Additional external data and repeated experiments remain future work.
